      Help

      Speech

      Select Read status to hear conditions, device status and sensor readings. Enable Auto announcements to hear state changes. You can stop speech during playback.

      Obstacle alerts

      Normal: green light and no obstacle alert. Near: yellow light, one beep and one vibration. Very close: red light, two beeps and two vibrations. Readings below the threshold are very close; below twice the threshold are near. Exit boundaries add half the threshold (at least 2 cm), capped at 400 cm. Entry and exit require three consecutive readings. Distance alerts take priority over fall vibration.

      Model & orientation

      Drag the model or use the arrow keys to adjust the view. Press Home to reset. The model is illustrative. Heading is relative and drifts over time.

      Event log

      Events use the time this computer received each change. A continuous fall is recorded once. After export, fill in Observed outcome to assess false alarms and missed detections.
